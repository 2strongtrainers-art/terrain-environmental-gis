Common questions

Do I need to understand AI?

No. Describe what you want in normal language. The goal comes first; the tool comes second.

Does every tool cost money?

No. The source collection includes free, free/paid, paid and not-yet-audited records. Pricing shown here comes from the source material or curated audit.

Does “no login” mean every free tool needs no account?

No. Only tools explicitly audited as no-login or optional-login are shown in the dedicated no-login collection. Unknown access is left unknown.

Can ChatGPT operate every website for me?

No. Some tasks can be completed directly in ChatGPT or through connected capabilities; other tasks require opening the specialist site.

Can I browse everything myself?

Yes. Use the Master Library for the cleaned index or Power User Mode for the four original source libraries.

🔒 A quick privacy note

Do not paste passwords, payment details, private client records, or highly sensitive information into an unfamiliar website. Review privacy terms before uploading sensitive files. For important medical, legal or financial decisions, verify information with a qualified professional.

Trust & transparency

Data quality

The Atlas separates “present in a source” from “live-verified.” Missing metadata stays unknown rather than being invented.

What each status means

Live verified means the current curated audit recorded a successful live-page verification. Source verified / Source listed means the website is present in an indexed source, not that every current feature or pricing claim has been re-tested.

Access type is only shown when the curated Notion database explicitly classified it. A free price does not automatically mean no account is required.

Historical Parts 1–749 include unresolved placeholders. Those rows are not converted into fake websites here; launchable URLs are indexed, while unresolved placeholders remain a QA issue in the underlying working directory.
